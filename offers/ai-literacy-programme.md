Article 4 of the EU AI Act asks every company that uses AI — any AI, at any risk level, including the features your software vendors switched on for you — to take measures so that the people who use it understand what it does and what it must not do. It has applied since 2 February 2025. There is no later deadline to wait for. And because it is a duty of means, what counts is what you can show you did: who was taught, what, and when.

Most companies have done nothing, for an ordinary reason: nobody owns it. It is not a legal question and not an IT question. It is a training question, and most training on AI is either a vendor demo or a webinar nobody remembers.

The **AI Literacy Programme** is the training, designed for your operation, with the record that proves it happened.

## What it covers

**One executive session** for management: what the law actually requires of a company like yours, what your people are already using — from your AI use register if you have one, or from a short survey if you don't — and the three or four decisions that follow. **Team sessions by function**: the people who use AI in sales do not need the same session as the people who use it in operations, finance or HR. Each one is built on their real tasks and tools, on what to rely on, what to check and what never to paste. **House rules** your teams helped write, so they get followed. And the **training record**: who attended, what was covered, when, and what they were asked to do next — the document you show when someone asks.

## How it works

**Before.** A short conversation with management and a look at what is in use. If the register from the Commercial Engine Assessment exists, it is the starting point.
**Delivery.** Sessions in person or online, in English, Spanish or Portuguese, within the working week. No e-learning platform, no video library.
**After.** The record, the house rules and a one-page note to management on what remains open. The programme is repeated when the tools or the people change — typically once a year.

Taught personally by Alexandre Pessoa, who has taught strategy, digital marketing and management in higher education for three decades and uses daily, in his own operation, the AI he teaches about.

## What this is not

It is not a legal opinion, and it does not make you "compliant". It gives you the measures the law asks for and the proof you took them. The reading of your specific obligations stays with your lawyers — and we will say so to them, too.

Any company operating in Portugal or Spain whose people use AI, in the open or quietly: subsidiaries of international groups whose head office has a policy nobody local has read, and Iberian industrial and technical companies where AI arrived inside the software before anyone decided it should.
