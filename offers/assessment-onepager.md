For a subsidiary that sells in Iberia what the group makes elsewhere, or for a technical company whose product is a service, the business *is* the commercial engine: how the market hears of you, how a lead becomes a customer, how that customer is served and kept, and how the people who do all this are hired and equipped. That is where digital and AI either pay or don't. It is also where most initiatives fail: tools bought and never used, pilots that never scale, processes nobody redesigned, people nobody brought along.

The **Commercial Engine Assessment** is a fixed-scope, four-week diagnostic that gives your leadership team a clear, prioritized answer to three questions: *Where do we stand? What is actually worth doing? And in what order?*

## What we examine

Six lenses on one engine, working directly with your management and the people who run it:

**Business context & strategy** — your market position, the group's priorities for Iberia, and what the commercial engine has to deliver. **Demand & sales** — how the market finds you and how a lead becomes a customer: presence, funnel, sales process — including whether AI assistants name you when buyers ask. **Processes & operations** — how marketing, sales, service and communications actually work, and where the friction is. **Data & systems** — CRM, analytics, automation: what talks to what, and what doesn't. **AI readiness** — every AI already in use, bought, embedded or unauthorised, and where AI would actually pay in your operation, and what must be true first. **People & adoption** — skills, habits and ownership: who will make the change stick.

## How it works

**Week 1 — Immersion.** Kickoff with leadership, structured interviews with key people, access to systems and data. Delivered by Friday: the **AI use register** — every AI in use, by function, owner and contract, with the AI Act obligation each one triggers, or none. The legal opinion stays with your lawyers; the inventory is yours.
**Weeks 2–3 — Analysis.** Audit of your presence, funnel, processes and toolset; benchmarking against your market, including what AI assistants say about you and your competitors.
**Week 4 — Roadmap & decision.** Findings consolidated into a prioritized roadmap, presented in person to your leadership team.

Fixed scope. Fixed timeline. Fixed fee. No open-ended engagement, no junior staff learning on your budget.
