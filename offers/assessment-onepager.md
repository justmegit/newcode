Most digital and AI initiatives don't fail because of the technology. They fail because nobody redesigns the processes around it, and nobody prepares the people who have to live with it. The result is familiar: tools that get bought but not used, pilots that never scale, and budgets spent proving what doesn't work.

The **Digital & AI Readiness Assessment** is a fixed-scope, four-week diagnostic that gives your leadership team a clear, prioritized answer to three questions: *Where do we stand? What is actually worth doing? And in what order?*

## What we examine

Over four weeks, working directly with your management and key people, we assess the six dimensions that determine whether transformation succeeds:

**Business context & strategy** — your market position, growth priorities and where digital should serve them. **Commercial engine** — how leads become customers today: digital presence, funnel, sales process. **Processes & operations** — how marketing, sales and delivery actually work, and where the friction is. **Data & systems** — what you have (CRM, analytics, automation), what talks to what, and what doesn't. **AI readiness** — where AI can create real leverage in *your* operation, and what must be true first. **People & adoption** — skills, habits and ownership: who will make the change stick.

## How it works

**Week 1 — Immersion.** Kickoff with leadership, structured interviews with key people, access to systems and data.
**Weeks 2–3 — Analysis.** Audit of your digital presence, funnel, processes and toolset; benchmarking against your market.
**Week 4 — Roadmap & decision.** Findings consolidated into a prioritized transformation roadmap, presented in person to your leadership team.

Fixed scope. Fixed timeline. Fixed fee. No open-ended engagement, no junior staff learning on your budget.
