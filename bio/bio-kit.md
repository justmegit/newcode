### The person behind the practice

Bluewright is led — and every engagement is conducted personally — by **Alexandre Pessoa**.

He was building digital firsts before "digital transformation" had a name: at Portugal's mobile operators he ran the world's first pilot of a WAP services portal, launched the world's first cross-platform mobile chat (2001), and created the product category that redefined internet access in Portugal — growing data services from zero to a fifth of operator revenue. Two lessons from all of it: strategy is cheap, and adoption is everything.

He works in **English, Spanish and Portuguese** (and gets by in French), moves comfortably between a board in Brussels and a sales team in Seville, and uses daily — in his own operation — the AI and automation he recommends.

No pyramid of juniors. No hours to sell. One senior partner, amplified by AI, accountable for the outcome.

## D. LONG BIO (~300 words — press, partner sites, detailed proposals)

Alexandre Pessoa has spent his whole career making new technology actually work inside organizations.

He began at sea: a merchant-navy radio officer responsible for every communications and safety system on board — an early lesson in technology where failure is not an option. As a sales engineer he then spent years selling scientific instrumentation to Portugal's most demanding technical buyers, from power plants to accredited laboratories.

At TMN and then Optimus, Portugal's mobile operators, he led product, innovation and new-business units through the founding decade of the mobile internet: the world's first pilot of a WAP services portal, the world's first cross-platform mobile chat (2001), Portugal's first operator JAVA games and Windows smartphones, the Kanguru mobile-broadband category that changed how the country went online — and the growth of data services from zero to roughly 20% of operator revenue. His teams were the first customer of Outsystems, today one of Portugal's global software successes.

In 2008 he founded his own digital practice, working as strategist and hands-on partner for B2B companies — Portuguese, Spanish, Belgian and American — from industrial SMEs to world leaders in aerospace and engineering, in relationships that typically last many years. That practice is today **Bluewright**: senior digital strategy and transformation, built on one conviction — technology only transforms a company when its processes are redesigned and its people brought along.

He holds a degree in electrical and telecommunications engineering, a Master's in Project Management, an executive MBA education, and Portugal's **Specialist Title in Business Sciences** (awarded by public examination, 2022). A higher-education lecturer for three decades and certified trainer, he works in English, Spanish and Portuguese, from Lisbon and Madrid.

## Notas (PT)

1. **Título de Especialista em EN:** usei "Portuguese state Specialist Title in Business Sciences, awarded by public examination" — formulação correta e impressiva sem overclaim. Não lhe chames "doctorate equivalent" em materiais públicos (é equivalente *para efeitos de docência politécnica*, não academicamente); a formulação por provas públicas já transmite o peso.
2. **Francês:** o teu CV 2022 autoavalia FR em B1/B2. Para clientes belgas (muitos são flamengos e trabalham em inglês, de resto), sugiro a formulação honesta que usei: "works in English, Spanish and Portuguese (and gets by in French)". Se entretanto o teu francês operacional é melhor que o CV sugere, repõe as quatro línguas — dizes-me.
3. **Testemunhos:** usei duas recomendações reais do teu LinkedIn com atribuição por função (sem nomes). Para o site, o ideal é pedires OK aos autores e usares nome+cargo — vale muito mais. São públicas no LinkedIn, mas a cortesia paga-se.
4. **Números telecom:** "zero a ~20% da receita" vem do teu CV com fontes (R&C Sonaecom). Sólido para usar.
5. **Cuidado com o certificado:** a digitalização do título contém o teu nº de identificação civil — nunca publiques a imagem; a menção textual chega.
6. O CV em si está em formato Europass e narrativa "agência full-service" — quando fecharmos a marca, vale a pena refazê-lo em formato consultor (2 páginas, EN). Fica na lista.
