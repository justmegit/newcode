## Attack memo

**1. "signed offers from paid-sourced contacts alone were worth 3.8x the month's media."** L21 is one signed deal, offer value not margin, from a contact created in August, when tracking was blind for 20 days (L12, L13). The piece calls that month "the month after the fixes", yet the consent fix was validated on 21 August. Credit is taken for a sale the fixes could not have caused.

**2. "maximising a constant."** If every lead carries one value, maximising value is maximising lead count, so "Moved bidding from value to volume" changes nothing. No row records the bidding setting, and "duplicate" is asserted, not shown.

**3. "The claim risk went to zero."** An absolute with no audit row. The replacement evidence is a 96-hour test where the ledger holds duration only. The diagnosis, "moved from the message to the path", rests on 16 landing-page views against 5 contacts (31%): a platform ratio Case 4 teaches readers to distrust.
