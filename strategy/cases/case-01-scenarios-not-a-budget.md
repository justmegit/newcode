# Scenarios, not a budget
*Challenge: "How much should we invest in Spain, and what would each level actually buy?"*
*Layer 2, Transform · draft v4, 24 Oct 2026 · anonymized*

---

**Context.** A building-products manufacturer from northern Europe, part of an industrial group, with an established catalogue and little traction in Spain. In that market the buying decision runs through prescribers: architects, installers and distributors, not end customers.

**The challenge.** Head office had to decide how much to put into Spain. The proposals on the table asked for a budget. None said what a given budget would buy, so the decision was either deferred or taken on faith.

**What was underneath.** The question was not "how much". It was "what are we buying". Advertising to end customers would have bought awareness among people who do not choose the product. What moves a specification is a prescriber with a reason to name the brand, and nobody in the company was giving them one.

**The decision.** We did not ask for a budget. We put two complete scenarios in front of the board, at two levels of media investment. Each set out the channels, what it would and would not produce, and the fee. At the centre of both sat a trade programme for prescribers: content, tools and incentives that give an architect or an installer a concrete reason to specify the brand.

**Where it stands.** The board has not decided. The question it faces has changed, though: it is no longer "how much should we spend?" but "which of these two outcomes do we want, and when?" That is a question a board can answer, and the work to answer it is done.

**What was harder than planned.** A clear choice does not set a date. Scenarios can show what each level buys; they cannot make a head office give one market priority over the others. The work should start by asking who decides, and by when.

---

**Three questions to ask your team tomorrow**
1. What is waiting costing you in that market, while prescribers build habits with someone else?
2. Who at head office owns this decision, and by what date?
3. Could your board pick between two scenarios today, or only approve or reject one number?
